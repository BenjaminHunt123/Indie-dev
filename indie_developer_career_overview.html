Development Lifecycle & Duties

The end-to-end process of solo and micro-studio indie game production.

01

Ideation & Greybox Prototyping

Designing game design documents (GDD), whiteboxing level layouts with primitive shapes, testing core gameplay loops, and verifying fun factor early.

Greyboxing & GDD
02

Architecture & Code Production

Writing clean, modular C# or C++ engine code, building state managers, UI logic, save systems, AI behavior, and optimizing data structures.

Clean Architecture
03

Asset & Audio Pipeline

Modeling/drawing 2D sprites or 3D low-poly meshes, rigging character animations, writing custom shaders, and mapping FMOD dynamic audio tracks.

Art & Audio Pipeline
04

Playtesting & QA Profiling

Profiling frame rates, memory allocation leak hunting, difficulty balancing curve, player feedback iterations, and fixing physics bugs.

QA & Profiling
05

Platform Build & Publishing

Steamworks SDK setup, store page capsule creation, achievements, cloud saves integration, mobile builds, and WebGL export for browser hosting.

Steam & Store Setup
06

Marketing & Post-Launch Support

Authoring developer logs (Devlogs), social teasers, press kits, Discord community management, post-release patch updates, and DLC content.

Devlogs & Patches
